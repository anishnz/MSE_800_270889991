QUESTION
========
Example: University and Department

Problem Statement
A university has several departments. Each department has a name and a
head of department. The university should be able to display its details
along with the details of its department.

Your task is to use composition to design this system.

Requirements
Create two classes:
    1. Department
         - Attributes: department_name, head
         - Method: show_department()
    2. University
         - Attributes: university_name
         - Create a Department object inside the University class.
         - Method: show_university() should display the university and
           department details.


ANSWER
======
Composition models a "has-a" relationship: a University HAS a Department,
rather than a University BEING a Department (which would instead be
inheritance, an "is-a" relationship).

Design:
    1. Department is a standalone class:
         - __init__(self, department_name, head): stores the department's
           own name and head of department.
         - show_department(): prints the department's name and head.

    2. University is composed of a Department:
         - __init__(self, university_name, department_name, head): stores
           university_name, and also CREATES a Department object right
           there, storing it as self.department. University does not
           inherit from Department -- it simply owns one as an attribute.
         - show_university(): prints the university's own name, then
           delegates to self.department.show_department() to print the
           department's details, rather than duplicating that display
           logic inside University.

    3. Usage:
           university = University("Yoobee Colleges", "Computer Science",
                                    "Dr. Jane Smith")
           university.show_university()

       Because University holds a Department object internally, any
       change to how a department displays itself only needs to be made
       in one place (Department.show_department()), and University keeps
       working correctly through delegation. This is the key benefit of
       composition over duplicating logic or forcing an inheritance
       relationship that doesn't reflect reality (a University is not a
       kind of Department).

Output when run:
    ---- University Details ----
    University: Yoobee Colleges
    Department: Computer Science
    Head of Department: Dr. Jane Smith


MORE QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS (FOR BETTER UNDERSTANDING)
=====================================================

Q1. What is composition, in simple words?
A1. Building a class by putting OTHER objects inside it. It is a
    "has-a" relationship: a University HAS a Department. The University
    stores a Department object as one of its attributes.

Q2. What is the difference between composition and inheritance?
A2. Inheritance = "is-a" (a Dog IS an Animal). Composition = "has-a"
    (a University HAS a Department). A University is not a kind of
    Department, so inheritance would be wrong here. Composition matches
    the real world.

Q3. Where exactly is the composition in the code?
A3. Inside University.__init__, a Department object is created and
    stored in self.department. From then on the University owns it.

Q4. What is delegation?
A4. Handing a job to the object that already knows how to do it.
    University.show_university() prints its own name, then calls
    self.department.show_department() to print the department details.
    University does not repeat the department's printing code.

Q5. What is the benefit of delegation?
A5. If the department display format changes, only
    Department.show_department() is edited. University keeps working
    without any change.

Q6. What is the difference between composition and aggregation?
A6. Both are "has-a".
    - Composition: the whole CREATES the part and owns it. If the
      University is deleted, its Department goes with it. (This
      exercise.)
    - Aggregation: the part is created OUTSIDE and passed in, for
      example University(name, existing_department_object). The part can
      exist without the whole.

Q7. A real university has many departments. How would the design
    change?
A7. Keep a LIST of Department objects, for example
    self.departments = [], add a method to add a department, and loop
    over the list in show_university():
        for department in self.departments:
            department.show_department()

Q8. Why is composition often preferred over inheritance?
A8. Objects are loosely connected. You can swap or change the part
    without touching the whole, and you avoid forcing classes into a
    parent-child relationship that does not really exist.
